# 8 Oct 2026: the overnight session

You asked for big visible changes overnight: Blender models, map detail, effects, gameplay, no
traffic. What landed on 8 Oct (all built, tested and pushed). Back to [[Changelog]].

## Blender kit
The city kit grew to 42 models, all drawn on every player's device with no import
([[Blender City Kit]]):
- Downtown: pickup, bus shelters, rooftop water towers and air units, food trucks, cafe tables, awnings, fountain, dumpsters, phone booths, traffic lights, post lamps, cypresses, shrubs, muscle car
- Hanami: city bus, commuter train (car and cab), utility poles, mamachari bikes, kei cars, taxis, vans, kei trucks, avenue lamps, torii, sakura, pines, stone lanterns, vending machines
- Pigeons for both cities
