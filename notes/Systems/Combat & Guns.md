# Combat & guns

Back to [[Home]] · related: [[Progression & Economy]], [[Enemies & AI]].

## Handling
- Moving and jumping open the cone; aiming steadies part of it. The crosshair shows the cone the server fires.
- **First-shot accuracy**: a rested gun fires its first round tighter. Snipers only get it scoped.
- **Tactical reloads** (a round still chambered) take 75% of the time.
- **Wallbangs**: rifles and snipers go through thin cover (glass, planks, crates); concrete stops everything.
- Crouch (hold C) tightens spread by a quarter.

## Tracers and hits
- Rounds leave the muzzle you see, fly at their class's speed, and land when they arrive.
- Headshots, damage numbers, hit flashes, kill bursts; Dark Matter hits play the gem-charge effect.
- Every shot also scares pedestrians and pigeons and can set off car alarms ([[City Life & Effects]]).

## VFX shelf (shop)
Five kill effects (Shatter, Confetti, Thunderstrike, Void, Gem Charge) and five tracers (Crimson,
Gold Rush, Plasma, Toxic, Prismatic). Chosen per gun in the inventory (✨ VFX).

## Mastery weapons
- **Carbine - Dark Matter** (+25% damage): Silver mastery, 100 kills with the Carbine
- **Warden - Dark Matter** (+33% damage): Gold mastery, 250 kills with the Warden (the HD rifle GLB)

## Attachments
Twelve, made for the Dark Matter Carbine: Red Dot, Holographic, 4x Scope, Compensator,
Suppressor, Vertical and Angled Grip, Bipod, Laser, Flashlight, Extended Mag, Skeleton Stock.
Bought and fitted in the Armory (K).

## Utility
Smoke and two medkits every life; frags from level 3, flashbangs from level 7; armour plates
dropped by enemies. Gear grades from Common to Mastery at ZEE TACTICS → [[Progression & Economy]].
