# Crime & heat

Downtown's street systems. Back to [[Home]] · map: [[Downtown]].

## Stick-ups
Hold **E** at a till (4 s) or ATM (6 s) in ZEE GUNS, ZEE TACTICS or the 24/7. You take a bag
and the heat: the alarm goes off, the server is told, a gold tag floats over you, and three of
the crew come after you. Hold the bag through the heat (60-75 s) and it is yours with 300 XP.
Targets stay empty for five minutes.

## The ZEE BANK truck
At the kerb up the west arm of the cross street (🚚). Hold E at the vault lock for 8 s: a
$1,600-2,400 bag, 90 s of heat, a bigger squad. Ten minutes between hits.

## Notoriety
Another job within ten minutes raises your stars (up to ★★★): more cash, more XP, longer heat,
bigger squads. Going down resets to one star.

## MOST WANTED
The BOUNTIES board names a crime boss (a Capo and two crew) every few minutes, plus every WANTED
player and their price. Bringing one in pays $1,500, XP, crystals and Street Cred.

## Turf wars
Four patches: THE CAR PARK, THE COURTS, THE ALLEY, THE PARK. Walk onto an open patch and its
crew comes out; clear them and everyone who helped is paid (MVP +50%). Each win raises the
**THREAT** (I-V): bigger crews, longer clocks, better pay. Won patches pay **tribute** every 90 s
until the crew **RAIDS** them back. Taking the fourth patch or winning at threat V brings out
**the Kingpin** (five minutes to beat them).

## Street Cred
+8 per war won (+4 as MVP), the Fixer's jobs, +20 for the Kingpin, -3 when a crew holds a patch
you fought for. Friendly (30+) pays 10% more from wars, Honoured (75+) 25% more.
