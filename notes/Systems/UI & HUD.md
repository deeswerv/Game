# UI & HUD

Back to [[Home]].

## On screen
- **Radar** (top right): turned the way you face, players white, crews red, bosses gold
- **Compass** (top): landmarks, your job as a gold diamond with distance, markers in the world
- **Area titles**: walk into a named place and its name comes up big
- **Downtown card**: THREAT and every patch's state
- Bottom-left: WANTED and heat badges, the stick-up bag's countdown, Hanami's raid bar
- Health with ticks every 10%, cyan armour, violet overshield; the streak meter over the weapon slots

## Menus and keys
Items (B), Shop (M), Armory (K), Profile (P), Daily (N), Quests (O), Contracts (L), Level road (Y),
Achievements (H), Travel (T), Settings (F1), Tab board, hold Alt to free the mouse.

## Joining
Loading screen over live Downtown → a ~20 s intro film (hold Space to skip) → the title screen
once per session (PLAY, 1V1 DUEL, DOWNTOWN, THE FLATS).

## Prompts
Every "press E" is the game's own key-cap prompt, outlined target, hold bar; robberies in red.
