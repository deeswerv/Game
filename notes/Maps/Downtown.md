# Downtown

The first city and where every player starts (Travel → **Downtown**, marked START HERE). A
cartoon low-poly street in saturated colours with white trim, no neon by day. Built by
`src/ServerScriptService/DistrictService/Downtown.luau`. Back to [[Home]].

![[notes/attachments/downtown-aerial.jpg]]

## Places
- **The avenue** from the welcome wall to **ZEE TOWER**, and **ZEE PLAZA** with its three-tier fountain
- **ZEE GUNS** (walk-in gun shop, workbench for attachments) and **ZEE TACTICS** (gear) with clerks
- **ZEE GAS** and the **24/7** store, with the **CONTRACTS** and **BOUNTIES** boards on its wall
- **The Fixer's corner** (purple JOBS canopy) south of the courts
- The **DUEL HALL** with its 1v1 pads
- **RAINBOW PARK**: waterfall, pond, the star-shaped ZEE BEATS stage
- **THE ALLEY** behind the gas station: fire escapes, dumpsters, the muscle car, a crew lives here
- The courts, a pocket park, the apartments (rooftop stash up the fire escape, once a day)
- **ZEE TOWER**: elevator to THE PENTHOUSE (level 8+), where **THE DON** waits

## What happens here
- Turf wars on four patches (THE CAR PARK, THE COURTS, THE ALLEY, THE PARK) → [[Crime & Heat]]
- Stick-ups at the tills and ATMs, the ZEE BANK truck, MOST WANTED → [[Crime & Heat]]
- The Fixer's job chain, Street Cred, the Kingpin → [[Progression & Economy]]
- Pedestrians, pigeons, steam, car alarms, night lights → [[City Life & Effects]]

## Kit models used here
Saloon, hatch, SUV, pickup, muscle car, taxi; crook lamps and post lamps; trees, cypresses,
shrubs; benches, bins, hydrants, dumpsters, phone booths, traffic lights, cafe tables; awnings;
the fountain; food trucks; bus shelters; rooftop water towers and air units. See [[Blender City Kit]].

## Notes and ideas
- Streets are kept sparse on purpose so the roads read and the tower stays in view.
- A bigger RPG district plan is written up in `docs/prompts/downtown-expansion.md`.
