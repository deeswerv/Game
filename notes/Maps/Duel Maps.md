# Duel maps

Press **J** (or stand on a 1V1 pad in the DUEL HALL) to queue. A pair who just played one map
gets another next, and two duels never share one. Each is lit its own way and has 180° symmetry
so both spawns see the same thing. One module per map in `ArenaService/`. Back to [[Home]].

| Map | Look |
|---|---|
| **Neon Court** | Rooftop court at night, magenta and cyan neon, crates and jersey barriers |
| **Dockyard** | Container quay at sunset, a gantry, a ship and cranes |
| **Rooftops** | Tar roof on a clear afternoon: stair huts, water towers, an HVAC deck |
| **Sakura Temple** | Temple courtyard at dusk: torii, koi pond, lanterns, pagodas |

Duels play like the reference: PLAY, VS screen, rounds with ROUND N resets, match point and
results. Both fighters get 50 armour a round and Common gear, so a duel stays even.
