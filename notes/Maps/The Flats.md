# The Flats

The stone courtyard between Map 2's four pillars, where the raiders and the town guard fight.
Built by `CoverService/Flats.luau`; every solid piece a person could crouch behind is tagged
`Cover`, which the AI's cover search prefers. Unlocked after the Don. Back to [[Home]].

- **Raider camp** (west): palisade, tents, campfire, log barricades; the Warlord waits outside the gate
- **Guard post** (east): crenellated wall, a climbable watchtower, a pavilion
- **The gatehouse** (centre): a ruined gateway across the line of fire
- **The walls** (north): the fast flank
- **The terrace** (south): the high ground over the open middle

Six raiders hold the camp. `tools/uitest/flats.luau` checks every post has room and the high
ground can be reached.
