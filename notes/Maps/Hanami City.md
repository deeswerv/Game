# Hanami City (chapter 2)

Opens on the Travel menu (🌸, LV 10-16) once the Don is beaten. A Japanese city 1,200 studs
square at a real city's scale next to the Roblox Boy body. Built by `HanamiService/City.luau`
(districts in `CitySouth`, `CityMiddle`, `CityNorth`; layout tools in `CityBlocks`; pieces in
`CityKit`; every position in `ReplicatedStorage/HanamiConfig.luau`). Back to [[Home]].

| Area | What is there |

| Kurogane Heights | The clan's glass towers round their plaza |

## The story (Elder Hoshi)
Lantern Row gate → clear Lantern Row → free three townsfolk → four clues in the Steelworks →
beat Foreman Tetsu → hold the fish market through three raids → five iron seals → climb the
Thousand Gates → **THE IRON SHOGUN**. First clear: $15,000, 8,000 XP, 40 crystals and the
**Shogun Slayer** title. Captain Rei pays bounties on the clan's specialists.

## The Kurogane clan
Ronin, Riflemen, Shieldbearers, Shinobi, Juggernauts and Oni → [[Enemies & AI]].
