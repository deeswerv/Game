# PROJECT ZEE

A Roblox RPG shooter: a cartoon low-poly city you fight for, duels on the side, and a story that
opens up a second city past the boss at the top of ZEE TOWER.

## Play the latest build
1. Download `build/Game.rbxl` from the `claude/roblox-gameplay-improvements-itfwjz` branch.
2. Roblox Studio → **File → Open from File…** → `Game.rbxl` → **Play**.
3. The **BUILD** stamp (title screen bottom-right, owner panel F4) tells you which build it is.

## At a glance
- In development since 25 Sep 2026; the latest game change is the ZEE BANK truck from the kit (13 Oct).
- 43 Blender models in the city kit, drawn on every player's device with no import.
- Two cities, four duel maps, the Flats, three bosses (the Kingpin, the Don, the Iron Shogun)
  and a mini-boss (Foreman Tetsu).
